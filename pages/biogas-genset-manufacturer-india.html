Laboratory-Scale Biogas Gensets for Research & Academic Applications

Our laboratory-scale biogas gensets are designed for universities, research institutions, and technical colleges conducting studies in renewable energy and waste-to-power conversion. Compact yet fully functional, these systems simulate industrial-scale setups with integrated biogas purification units, control panels, and measurement instrumentation.

They are ideal for training students, testing gas compositions, and studying engine performance, efficiency, and emission characteristics—making them the perfect educational tool for the next generation of green engineers.

Portable Biogas Gensets for Village Electrification & Farm Power

Enersol Biopower’s portable biogas gensets bring clean, affordable energy to rural and agricultural areas. By utilizing cow dung, kitchen waste, and agricultural residues, these units generate reliable electricity for lighting, irrigation, and small machinery.

Compact and easy to move, they provide daytime power for pumps and nighttime power for homes, empowering farmers to use their own biogas for productive purposes.

This plug-and-play renewable energy solution transforms waste into wealth—enhancing rural livelihoods and contributing to India’s clean energy mission.

Biogas Gensets for Electricity Generation — Replacing Diesel Gensets in Industries

Many agro-industries, cold storage units, and telecom sites rely on diesel generator sets for electricity. Enersol Biogas Gensets provide a cleaner, cheaper, and more sustainable replacement—running entirely on biogas from organic waste.

Designed for continuous duty operation, these systems deliver consistent 5–250 kVA power outputs with minimal maintenance. Users benefit from reduced diesel costs, lower emissions, and freedom from fuel supply disruptions.

Whether for industrial processing, farm electrification, or community microgrids, our gensets ensure dependable renewable power 24×7.

Industrial Biogas Gensets for Continuous Power & Process Energy

Enersol’s industrial biogas gensets are engineered for high-efficiency power generation in manufacturing units, food processing plants, and agro-industries. They can operate continuously, ensuring uninterrupted production even in off-grid locations.

Our systems are equipped with advanced gas conditioning, low-NOx engines, and auto-synchronization options for integration with existing electrical systems.

Industrial users achieve up to 70% energy cost savings compared to diesel while reducing their carbon footprint and meeting ESG sustainability goals.

Laboratory-Scale Biomass Gasifiers for Research & Academic Applications

Our laboratory-scale biomass gasifiers are engineered for universities, research centers, and technical institutes. These compact units are true replicas of large industrial gasifiers, equipped with all essential features such as gas cleaning filters, thermocouples for temperature monitoring, and a generator for electricity production. They provide an ideal platform for studying gasification processes, testing fuel performance, and training students in renewable energy.

Portable Biomass Gasifier for Village Electrification & Farm Irrigation

Over 700 million people worldwide still live without electricity, while millions of tons of agricultural residues and woody biomass are discarded or openly burned, causing pollution. Enersol Biopower’s portable biomass gasifier bridges this gap by converting locally available biomass into clean, reliable energy.

Lightweight and easy to operate, it delivers dual benefits—powering irrigation pumps during the day and lighting homes at night. By turning waste into wealth, our system reduces dependence on costly fossil fuels, supports sustainable farming, and improves rural livelihoods.

More than just a machine, it is a practical solution for energy access and rural transformation—helping communities worldwide harness their biomass abundance for a brighter, self-reliant future.

Biomass Gasifier for Electricity Generation — Replacing Diesel Gensets in Agro & Tower Industries

In many remote areas, agro-industries, cold storage farms, telecom towers, and other tower industries rely heavily on diesel generator sets (DG sets) to meet their electricity needs. These DG sets not only incur high fuel costs but also contribute to significant greenhouse gas emissions and require frequent maintenance and fuel transport logistics. A 5–250 kVA biomass gasifier can serve as a sustainable alternative, capable of replacing diesel gensets for typical loads in agro-processing, irrigation, cold storage, and telecom operations.

Fueled by locally available biomass waste—such as crop residues, husks, and agro-industrial by-products—these gasifiers provide reliable electricity day and night, reducing dependence on costly diesel and improving energy security. In addition to cutting fuel costs, biomass gasifiers also help manage agricultural waste, lower carbon emissions, and offer a cleaner, more sustainable energy solution for industries operating in villages and off-grid regions.

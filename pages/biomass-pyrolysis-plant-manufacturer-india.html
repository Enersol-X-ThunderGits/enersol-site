Laboratory-Scale Biomass Pyrolysis Plant for Research & Training

Industrial-Scale Biomass Pyrolysis Plants for Continuous Biochar Production

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Who is the leading biomass pyrolysis plant manufacturer in India? + -

A: Enersol Biopower is one of India’s leading manufacturers and exporters of biomass pyrolysis and biochar plants. With over a decade of experience, we provide turnkey solutions for biomass conversion, carbon capture, and sustainable soil enhancement applications.

Q2: What is a biomass pyrolysis plant used for? + -

A: A biomass pyrolysis plant converts agricultural residues, wood waste, and organic matter into biochar, syngas, and bio-oil. Biochar is used to improve soil fertility, sequester carbon, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions, making pyrolysis a vital part of sustainable agriculture and waste management.

Q3: What is the price range of a biomass pyrolysis or biochar plant in India? + -

A: The cost of a biomass pyrolysis plant depends on its production capacity and level of automation. Enersol Biopower offers models from 5 kg/hr to 500 kg/hr, suitable for farms, research institutions, and industrial clients. Contact us for a customized quotation based on your application and feedstock type.

Q4: What are the main applications of biochar produced from these plants? + -

A: Biochar is widely used for soil enrichment, water retention, carbon sequestration, and waste remediation. It enhances crop yields, improves soil structure, and reduces the need for chemical fertilizers, making it highly valuable for farmers, agro-industries, and environmental projects.

Q5: Are Enersol Biopower biochar plants eco-friendly? + -

A: Yes. Our Eco-Green Biochar series is designed with low-emission, energy-efficient pyrolysis technology. These plants operate with minimal smoke and no harmful discharge, helping users convert waste into valuable carbon-based products while protecting the environment.

Q6: Does Enersol Biopower export biomass pyrolysis plants internationally? + -

A: Absolutely. Enersol Biopower is a reputed exporter of biomass pyrolysis and biochar systems to Asia, Africa, and the Middle East. Our export models are CE-compliant and meet global standards for renewable and sustainable energy production.

What is a Smokeless Biomass Stove?

A smokeless biomass stove uses gasification-based combustion, with natural draft or a small fan (forced draft), to burn agricultural residues, wood chips, or briquettes with no smoke when dry fuel is used. It replaces LPG and traditional chulhas, saving up to 60% (typically 50–70%) on cooking fuel costs while eliminating harmful indoor air pollution for homes, dhabas, and institutions.

Biomass Stove FAQs

What is a biomass stove and how does it work?

+ -

A biomass stove burns wood chips, crop residue, briquettes or coconut shells instead of LPG. Enersol stoves use gasification-based combustion: the fuel first releases combustible gas, and a second stage burns that gas with extra air, so no smoke escapes when dry fuel is used. Forced-draft models use a fan or blower for a steady, adjustable flame.

How much can I save with a biomass stove compared to LPG?

+ -

Most users save up to 60% on cooking-fuel cost (typically 50–70%, depending on LPG price, fuel cost and daily use). High-usage commercial models such as Green-Magic15 can pay back in as little as 25 days; a typical payback is 3–6 months.

What are the types of biomass stoves?

+ -

There are three main types of biomass stoves. A natural-draft stove draws in air through the chimney effect and needs no fan; Enersol's Bio-Nature home stove is this type. A forced-draft stove uses a small 12V DC fan to give a steady flame you can adjust; this covers Bio-Mini, Bio-Mini Solar and the commercial range from Green-Agnika10 to Jumbo-Shakti36. A gasifier stove burns the fuel gas in a second stage, so dry fuel gives no smoke, and all Enersol forced-draft models work this way. Every Enersol model is multi-fuel and also runs on biomass pellets and briquettes.

Which fuels can I use in an Enersol biomass stove?

+ -

Dry biomass with moisture below 20%: wood chips, firewood, crop residue, coconut shells, biomass pellets, sawdust briquettes and garden waste.

Can I use pellets and briquettes in Enersol stoves?

+ -

Yes, pellets and briquettes are suitable for use in Enersol biomass stoves, provided they are made from dry biomass with moisture below 20%.

Which biomass stove is best for home use?

+ -

For homes, Bio-Nature (natural draft, cooks for 5–25 people) and Bio-Mini (12V DC fan, 1.5–2 kg/hr fuel use) are the usual choices. Bio-Mini Solar adds a 10W solar panel and LED light for homes with unreliable power.

Which biomass stove should a hotel, dhaba, sweet shop or institution buy?

+ -

Small stalls and street-food vendors use Green-Agnika10 or Harit-Agni12. Dhabas, sweet shops and canteens use Green-Magic15, Bio-Virat18 (18-inch) or Bio-Akash21 (21-inch). Large community kitchens, temples and hostels use Bio-Prithvi24, Harit-Jumbo27 or Jumbo-Shakti36 (36-inch).

Do biomass stoves produce smoke?

+ -

Enersol stoves produce no smoke when dry fuel is used. Wet fuel (above 20% moisture) increases smoke, so keep fuel dry.

Lakdi ki bhatti kya hoti hai aur kya yeh biomass stove se better hai?

+ -

Lakdi ki bhatti ek traditional wood-burning stove hai jo dhabas, hotels aur commercial kitchens mein use hoti hai, par usme dhuan zyada hota hai aur lakdi ki khapat bhi zyada. Enersol ki smokeless biomass bhatti dry fuel par dhuan nahi deti, up to 60% kam fuel use karti hai aur LPG se sasta khana pakati hai.

Do you provide installation, training and after-sales support?

+ -

Yes. Enersol provides installation assistance, operator training and after-sales technical support across India, and exports biomass stoves to Africa, the Middle East and Asia.

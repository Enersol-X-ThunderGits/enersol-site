What is a Smokeless Biomass Stove?

A smokeless biomass stove uses forced-draft combustion technology to burn agricultural residues, wood chips, or briquettes with near-zero smoke. It replaces LPG and traditional chulhas, saving 50–70% on cooking fuel costs while eliminating harmful indoor air pollution for homes, dhabas, and institutions.

What is a Wood Stove (Biomass Stove)?

A wood stove — also called a wood-burning stove or biomass stove — burns firewood, wood chips, agricultural waste, or biomass briquettes to generate powerful, consistent heat for cooking.

Enersol Biopower's advanced smokeless wood stove takes this traditional concept further with forced-draft technology, an optimised combustion chamber, and clean-burning design — producing zero visible smoke while consuming 60% less fuel than a traditional wood bhatti or LPG setup.

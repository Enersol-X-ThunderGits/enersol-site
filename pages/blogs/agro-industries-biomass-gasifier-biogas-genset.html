Ready to Switch to Renewable Energy Solutions?

When it comes to sustainable fuel management for agro-based businesses, Enersol Biopower stands out as a trusted Biomass Gasifier Manufacturer and Biogas Genset Manufacturer with over 25 years of field-proven installations across rice mills, dal mills, oil expellers, and agro-processing units across India. As a leading Biomass Gasifier Supplier in India, Enersol Biopower designs gasifier systems from 2 kW to 250 kW that run entirely on locally available crop residues — eliminating dependency on diesel and LPG at operating sites.

For units that generate organic waste — spent wash, cattle dung, or agro-industry effluent — Enersol Biopower's role as a Biogas Genset Supplier becomes equally important. Biogas gensets convert anaerobic digester output directly into electricity and thermal energy, creating a closed-loop energy system within the agro-industrial complex. These systems are also available for international buyers — Enersol Biopower serves as a Biomass Gasifier Exporter and Biogas Genset Exporter with completed projects in Africa, the Middle East, and South Asia. Additionally, agro units generating large volumes of biomass residue can benefit from Enersol's Pyrolysis Plant range: as a Pyrolysis Plant Manufacturer and Pyrolysis Plant Supplier, the company converts agro-waste into high-value biochar, bio-oil, and syngas — turning waste disposal costs into a revenue stream.

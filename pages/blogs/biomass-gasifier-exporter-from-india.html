What Is a Biomass Gasifier?

A biomass gasifier is a specialized machine that converts agricultural residues — such as paddy straw, wood chips, coconut shells, maize cobs, and husks — into producer gas, a clean-burning fuel.

This gas can:

  • Generate electricity for homes, villages, and industries.
  • Provide heat for cooking, crop drying, and industrial processes.
  • Power machinery in small and medium factories.

It’s cheaper than diesel or LPG and produces far fewer harmful emissions. Think of it as a “smart fire” in a sealed chamber — capturing and cleaning gases instead of releasing smoke into the air.

Across Africa, Asia, and Latin America, biomass gasifiers are helping farming cooperatives and rural communities:

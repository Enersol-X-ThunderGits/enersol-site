Ready to Switch to Renewable Energy Solutions?

Dairy farms sit at the intersection of two powerful clean energy feedstocks: cattle dung for biogas and crop residue for biomass — and Enersol Biopower is uniquely positioned to convert both into usable energy. As a seasoned Biogas Genset Manufacturer and Biogas Genset Supplier, Enersol Biopower designs genset systems specifically for dairy farms — from compact 2 kVA units for small dairies to 50 kVA systems for large cooperative plants — that run on biogas produced from cattle dung digesters. These systems provide electricity for milking machines, cooling compressors, water pumps, and lighting — dramatically cutting the farm's electricity bill from day one.

For farms that also generate paddy straw, sugarcane bagasse, or other dry crop residue, Enersol's role as a Biomass Gasifier Manufacturer and Biomass Gasifier Supplier in India enables additional electricity generation from a parallel biomass gasification system. The Biomass Stove for Rural Kitchens range can serve the farm workers' canteen, replacing LPG or the traditional Wood Stove setup. As a Biogas Genset Exporter, Enersol Biopower has also supplied dairy farm biogas systems to international buyers in Africa and the Middle East. Dairy farmers interested in maximising value from manure solids and biomass residue can explore Enersol's Pyrolysis Plant range — as a Pyrolysis Plant Supplier, Enersol converts dung cake and biomass waste into biochar that improves pasture soil fertility and reduces fertiliser costs.

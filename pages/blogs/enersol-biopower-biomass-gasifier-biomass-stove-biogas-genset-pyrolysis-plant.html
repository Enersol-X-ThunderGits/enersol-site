Ready to Transform Your Energy Costs?

Enersol Biopower is not just a manufacturer — it is a complete clean energy solutions partner serving industries, institutions, farms, and rural communities across India and worldwide. As a Biomass Gasifier Manufacturer and Biomass Gasifier Supplier in India, the company offers gasifier systems from 2 kW to 250 kW for electricity generation, process heat, and industrial thermal applications — all running on agricultural residue that would otherwise be burnt or discarded. As a Biomass Gasifier Exporter, Enersol has successfully deployed systems at prestigious institutions including IIT Ropar, NIT Tiruchirappalli, KNUST Ghana, and UNDP Yemen.

In the cooking energy segment, Enersol Biopower is a trusted Biomass Cooking Stove Manufacturer and Biomass Stove Exporter, offering forced-draft and natural-draft stoves for homes, schools, ashrams, dhabas, and industrial kitchens. Their Biomass Stove for Rural Kitchens range replaces smoky Wood Stove and LPG setups with clean, efficient biomass combustion. As a Biogas Genset Manufacturer, Biogas Genset Supplier, and Biogas Genset Exporter, Enersol converts organic waste — from cattle dung to food waste — into reliable electricity for farms, hotels, and industries. Finally, as a Pyrolysis Plant Manufacturer, Pyrolysis Plant Supplier, and Pyrolysis Plant Exporter, Enersol Biopower helps agro-industries, waste processors, and farms transform biomass and plastic waste into biochar, bio-oil, and syngas — creating circular economies that generate income, improve soil health, and reduce carbon emissions simultaneously.

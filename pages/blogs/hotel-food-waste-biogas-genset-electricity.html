Hotel Food Waste Problem

Hotels, restaurants, and resorts generate significant quantities of food waste every day. Leftover food, kitchen scraps, vegetable waste, and expired ingredients often become landfill waste.

This creates environmental problems, waste disposal costs, and methane emissions from uncontrolled decomposition.

How Biogas Gensets Work

Organic waste is fed into a biogas digester where anaerobic bacteria break down the waste and produce methane-rich biogas.

Organic Waste

Food waste collected from hotel kitchens.

Biogas Production

Methane-rich gas produced through digestion.

Electricity Generation

Biogas powers gensets for electricity generation.

Electricity Generation Process

The generated biogas is filtered and supplied to a Biogas Genset engine. The genset converts gas energy into electrical energy.

Hotels can use this electricity for lighting, kitchen equipment, air conditioning, and other operations.

Benefits for Hotels

Lower Electricity Bills

Hotels reduce dependence on grid electricity and diesel generators.

Sustainable Branding

Eco-friendly hotels attract environmentally conscious travelers.

Waste Management

Food waste disposal problems are reduced significantly.

Hotels across the world are adopting renewable energy systems to reduce environmental impact and improve operational efficiency.

Biogas Gensets will become an important technology for sustainable hospitality infrastructure in the future.

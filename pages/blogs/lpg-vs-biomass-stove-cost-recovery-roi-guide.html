The Cost Comparison: LPG vs. Biomass

Sabse pehle calculation samajhte hain. Maan lijiye aap ek commercial kitchen ya bada parivar hain jo rozana 1 commercial LPG cylinder (19kg) ya uske barabar gas consume karte hain.

LPG ka Hisab (Monthly)

  • Average Cost (Commercial): ₹1,800 – ₹2,100 per cylinder
  • Monthly Expense (30 Days): ~₹54,000 – ₹60,000
  • Price control: Zero — global market linked

Biomass ka Hisab (Monthly)

  • Cost of Briquettes: ₹12 – ₹15 per kg
  • Daily Cost: ₹240 – ₹300
  • Monthly Expense: ~₹7,200 – ₹9,000

Direct Saving: Aap har mahine ₹45,000 se ₹50,000 tak bacha sakte hain! Agar aapka consumption kam bhi hai, toh bhi savings ka ratio yahi rahega.

3-Month Recovery Plan: Ye Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

Humne is 3-month recovery ko teen stages mein divide kiya hai:

M1

Month 1: The Transition Phase

Pehle mahine mein aap stove chalana seekhte hain aur fuel sourcing (briquettes/pellets) set karte hain. Is mahine mein aap gas ki dependence ko 80% tak kam kar dete hain. Aapka gas bill ₹50,000 se gir kar ₹10,000 par aa jata hai.

M2

Month 2: Optimization

Ab aapka staff ya parivar is stove ka expert ban chuka hai. Aap thermal heat ka sahi upyog kar rahe hain. Is mahine ki bachat se aapne stove ki 70% cost nikal li hai.

M3

Month 3: Breaking Even

Teesre mahine ke khatam hote hi, stove ki poori cost vasool! Ab aapka kitchen "Energy Independent" hai. 2026 ke trends ke hisab se, jo log biomass par shift ho chuke hain, unki business profitability 25% badh gayi hai.

Kaunse Businesses ke liye ye "Must-Have" Hai?

Agar aap niche diye gaye kisi bhi kaam se jude hain, toh aap har guzarte din ke saath paisa kho rahe hain:

Dhabas & Restaurants

Rozana ki frying aur cooking mein bhari bachat — daily operation mein LPG replace karna sabse fast ROI deta hai.

Halwai & Sweet Shops

Mawa banane aur chashni ubalne ke liye best — constant high-heat demand makes biomass switch most impactful.

Hostels & Canteens

Bulk cooking mein LPG ka kharcha aadha — 100+ logon ke liye khana banane mein biomass maximum savings deliver karta hai.

Dairy Farms

Doodh garam karne aur processing ke liye — farm waste ko fuel ki tarah use karke cost near-zero ho sakti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Biomass stove kitna mehenga hota hai?

Enersol Biopower ke commercial biomass stoves ki price range ₹15,000 se ₹60,000 tak hoti hai, model aur capacity ke hisab se. Lekin yeh ek baar ka investment hai — monthly savings se yeh 2–3 mahine mein vasool ho jata hai.

Q2: Biomass stove mein kaunsa fuel use kar sakte hain?

Aap biomass briquettes, wood pellets, lakdi ke tukde, coconut shells, agricultural waste jaise gehu ki naari aur makke ke thunth use kar sakte hain. Enersol recommend karta hai ki fuel sookha ho aur 20% se kam moisture ho.

Q3: Kya biomass stove se khane ka taste change hota hai?

Nahi. Balki bahut se log kehte hain ki biomass stove se bana khana zyada achha lagta hai kyunki yeh traditional slow-burn heat deta hai — bilkul waise jaise purane chulhe ya wood-fire mein hota tha, lekin bina dhuaan ke.

Q4: 3-month recovery plan kaise kaam karta hai?

Pehle mahine mein transition aur gas dependence 80% kam, doosre mahine mein optimization aur stove ki 70% cost recover, teesre mahine ke end tak poori cost vasool aur kitchen energy-independent. Uske baad har mahine ki savings pure profit hai.

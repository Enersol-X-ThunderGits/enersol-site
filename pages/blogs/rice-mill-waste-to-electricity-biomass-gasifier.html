Introduction

Rice mills produce large quantities of rice husk every day. Traditionally, this agricultural waste was either dumped, burned openly, or disposed of inefficiently.

Modern Biomass Gasifier technology changes this completely by converting rice husk into producer gas, which is then used to generate electricity or industrial heat.

What is Rice Husk?

Rice husk is the outer shell removed during rice milling. It is lightweight, combustible, and rich in biomass energy.

Biomass Energy Potential

Rice husk contains significant calorific value, making it suitable for electricity generation using Biomass Gasifiers.

How Biomass Gasifiers Work

Biomass Gasifiers convert solid biomass fuel into combustible producer gas through a controlled thermal process called gasification.

Thermal Conversion

Biomass is heated in low oxygen conditions.

Producer Gas

Combustible gas powers engines and generators.

Renewable Energy

Waste becomes electricity and heat.

Benefits for Rice Mills

Reduced Diesel Costs

Rice mills reduce fuel expenses by replacing diesel generators with biomass electricity systems.

Waste Utilization

Agricultural waste becomes a valuable energy resource.

Sustainability

Renewable biomass energy reduces carbon emissions and environmental impact.

Future of Rice Mill Renewable Energy

As energy prices continue to rise, more rice mills are expected to adopt renewable biomass energy technologies.

Biomass Gasifiers will play a major role in sustainable agro-industrial growth across India and global agricultural markets.

Ready to Reduce Fuel Costs with Renewable Energy?

Millions of rural kitchens across India still rely on open firewood or costly LPG — both of which carry high economic and health costs for rural households and small eateries. Enersol Biopower, a dedicated Biomass Cooking Stove Manufacturer, has spent over two decades solving exactly this problem. Their Biomass Stove for Rural Kitchens range includes nature-draft models for home use and forced-draft industrial models for larger rural establishments, all of which eliminate smoke, cut fuel costs by 50–70%, and run on locally sourced biomass — from crop stubble to coconut shells — instead of expensive commercial fuels.

For rural kitchens that need electricity alongside cooking energy, Enersol Biopower's Biogas Genset systems — the company is a certified Biogas Genset Manufacturer and Biogas Genset Supplier — convert cattle dung or kitchen waste into reliable off-grid electricity. Their Biomass Gasifier range, available as a Biomass Gasifier Supplier in India, further expands this capability by generating electricity from dry biomass like rice husk or wood chips. For rural entrepreneurs looking to add income streams, Enersol's Pyrolysis Plant — supplied through its role as a Pyrolysis Plant Supplier — converts farm waste into marketable biochar and bio-oil. Together, these solutions help rural kitchens and agro-enterprises build complete energy independence at the lowest possible cost.

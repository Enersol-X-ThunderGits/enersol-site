Why Power Cuts Matter in the Kitchen

Many clean cooking options depend on electricity in some way. Induction cooktops and electric cookers stop during a power cut. Standard forced-draft stoves need power for their fan as well, because the fan is what makes the fuel burn cleanly.

In areas with unreliable supply, the fallback is often the old chulha, and a kerosene lamp once the sun goes down. That means smoke in the kitchen, fumes from the lamp and money spent on both. A stove that works with a small solar setup is designed for exactly these homes.

What Is a Solar Biomass Stove?

The name can be confusing, so let us be clear. This is not a solar cooker. A solar cooker uses sunlight as its heat, and it cannot cook at night or under a heavy cloud.

A solar biomass stove burns biomass for heat. The solar panel is a small electrical source, not a heater. In the Bio-Mini Solar, you get three things in one product:

1
A smokeless stove: A forced-draft stove with an enclosed combustion chamber, fed from the top.
2
A 12V DC fan: It pushes air into the fire for a cleaner burn, and the regulator lets you adjust the flame.
3
A solar panel and LED light: A 10W panel and a single LED light the home after dark.

How Much Fuel Will You Need?

The stove uses about 1.5–2 kg of biomass an hour. Take a family that cooks for 2 hours in the morning and 1.5 hours in the evening.

3.5 hours × 1.5–2 kg = about 5–7 kg of biomass a day
5–7 kg × 30 days = about 150–210 kg a month

This is only an illustration, and your real use will depend on how much you cook. If you have your own crop residue, wood pieces or pellets, your fuel may cost little. Keep it dry and under cover, because the stove needs fuel below 20% moisture. Our guide on turning garden and farm waste into fuel has practical tips.

Getting the Most from the Solar Panel

Enersol's product data lists up to 12 hours of lighting. A 10W panel is small, so a good position makes a real difference.

  • Find full sun: Place the panel where it gets direct sun for most of the day. In India, facing it south usually works best.
  • Avoid shade: Even the shadow of a tree or a wall can cut its output.
  • Keep it clean: Wipe away dust and bird droppings with a soft, damp cloth.
  • Protect the wire: Run the cable where it will not be stepped on or chewed.
  • Plan for cloudy days: Expect shorter lighting time in the monsoon.

Treat the panel as a small helper for this stove and its light, not as a home power system. It is not meant to run fans, televisions or other household appliances. For cleaning and common faults, see our biomass stove daily care guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a solar biomass stove?

It is a smokeless biomass stove that comes with a small solar setup. Enersol's Bio-Mini Solar combines a forced-draft stove with a 10W solar panel and an LED light, so one product supports both cooking and lighting.

Does a solar biomass stove cook with solar energy?

No. The heat comes from burning biomass such as wood pellets and agricultural residue. The solar panel is a small electrical source, and it does not produce cooking heat. This is different from a solar cooker, so the stove can cook after dark or on a cloudy day.

How much fuel does the Bio-Mini Solar use?

The Bio-Mini Solar uses about 1.5–2 kg of biomass an hour. A home cooking for 3.5 hours a day would need about 5–7 kg a day. Fuel moisture should stay below 20%.

How many people can the Bio-Mini Solar cook for?

It is listed for preparing meals for 5–25 people. That makes it suitable for homes, tea stalls and small community cooking. Larger kitchens should look at bigger institutional stoves.

How long does the solar LED light last?

Enersol's product data lists lighting for up to 12 hours. The actual time will depend on how much sun the panel received, so keep the panel clean and in full sun during the day.

Can I replace an LPG stove with a solar biomass stove?

Many homes can use it as a main stove or as a backup. It suits homes with access to dry biomass. Compare your LPG cost, your local fuel price, the time needed to feed the stove and your storage space before you decide.

Ready to Reduce Fuel & Electricity Costs?

Rural canteens and dhabas that operate without reliable grid power have found a dependable ally in Enersol Biopower — a trusted Biogas Genset Manufacturer and Biogas Genset Supplier serving village-level businesses across India. Enersol's compact 2 kVA to 15 kVA biogas gensets are designed to run on biogas produced from kitchen waste, animal dung, or local organic material — giving village canteens an uninterrupted and nearly free source of electricity. As a Biogas Genset Exporter, Enersol Biopower has also deployed similar off-grid power systems in rural communities in Africa and South Asia, proving the model at scale.

For cooking needs within the same canteen, Enersol Biopower offers Biomass Stove for Rural Kitchens — forced-draft, smokeless stoves that burn agricultural residue or wood at dramatically lower cost than LPG. Village canteens that also have access to crop residue from nearby farms can install a small Biomass Gasifier for additional power generation, as Enersol Biopower is a certified Biomass Gasifier Manufacturer and Biomass Gasifier Supplier in India with portable models starting at 2 kW. For waste-to-value applications, Enersol's Pyrolysis Plant range — backed by its reputation as a Pyrolysis Plant Supplier — can convert biomass and organic waste into biochar that local farmers can use to improve soil fertility, completing the circular economy loop for the entire village.
